WHY SUSTAINO

Packaging decisions should be easier to make.

Sustaino is built around a simple idea: useful packaging information should be easy to browse before you enquire. We help customers navigate product formats, material groups and service needs without treating every item as the same.

Three ways to browse

Start with the product you need, the industry you work in, or the material group you prefer. Each path takes you to the same catalogue from a more useful starting point.

Clear item identity

Every listing is kept separate by SKU. Containers, bases and lids are not merged into one product, so it is easier to request the exact item you mean.

Material transparency

Plant-based fibre, recycled paper, PET and PP items are grouped clearly. Material labels come from product information and are shown product by product.

Practical support

Use LINE to share your SKU, service type and delivery location. We can then help you narrow down suitable formats without showing unverified claims or prices online.
