ABOUT SUSTAINO PACKAGING TH

Better packaging choices, made practical for everyday service.

Sustaino Packaging TH helps foodservice businesses compare packaging by product format, material and the way they serve. We bring clear product information into one place, so restaurants, cafés, delivery teams and dessert businesses can start the right conversation.

What we do

We curate foodservice packaging formats for Thailand—from meal boxes and soup bowls to cups, lids, trays and service accessories. Each product stays identifiable by its own SKU so teams can enquire accurately.

How we help

Instead of making customers search through a long technical list, we organise the collection three ways: by product type, by business industry and by material group.

Clear catalogue

Product pages show the information supplied for each item: SKU, dimensions, material and carton quantity where available.

Designed for service

Find practical formats for dine-in, takeaway, drinks, delivery and dessert presentation in one catalogue.

Direct enquiry
